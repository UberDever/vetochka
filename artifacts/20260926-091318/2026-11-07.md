## Confrontation and open questions

- Analysis (agent): pleasant literal notation does not require adding an observable primitive kind. A parser can lower rich literals into either the old representation or the proposed one. Decide the value algebra separately from its notation.
- Analysis (agent): the proposed model can keep strings and numbers as interpreted/tagged byte payloads. Raw bytes alone do not determine whether a payload means text, an integer, or something else. Encoding and validity remain conventions or operation contracts.
- Open (agent): specify how programs distinguish Nil, Pair, and Bytes and what operations expose byte contents. Native Bytes would change the homogeneous-nyad observation model, not merely storage.
- Open (agent): is this an algebra of observable value descriptions, or an exhaustive model of runtime objects? A byte encoding of a native handle still requires rules connecting it to a live resource. Copying the bytes does not reconstruct that resource.
- Impact (agent): the proposed constructors omit the current primitive stem. Its syntax and observation would need removal or an agreed encoding if the proposal is selected.

## Selected starting point

- Direction (user): start from Nil, Pair, and Bytes and the interpreter operations for them. Applications, lambdas, and other executable forms use list encodings interpreted by rules that remain to be written.
- Rationale (user): bytes are directly observable payloads, not hidden storage behind a claim that everything decomposes into Nil and Pair. Inspection retains three cases, with Bytes distinct from Nil.
- Direction (user): staging keeps encoded terms inspectable and constructible as ordinary lists.
