Source: https://github.com/UberDever/crosslingual-analysis/blob/master/projects/crossy/lsp-adapter/examples/ontology/types/constructors.js (blob `9fdf5968a88794e2b329378f77d9aa4af5e63006`).

Consumers still inspect ordinary trees, while shared builders reduce independent reimplementation of the convention. Validators can check terms constructed through other paths.
