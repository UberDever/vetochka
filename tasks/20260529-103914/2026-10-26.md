## Lambda calculus and homogeneous trees

- Model (user): Vetochka is a lambda-calculus-based language built from homogeneous trees. Trees direct computation. Triage calculus is no longer the language basis; homogeneous trees and triage are the retained ideas.
- Direction (user): remove primitive rules 0, 1, and 2. Construction from former rules 0a and 0b is expressible through lambdas. The user's example replaces `(~)(~)` with `(\[x] do ~[x] end)(~)`.
- Proposal (user): only applications reduce. Lower `=[]` and other executable forms to applications. The callees and exact lowering remain unspecified.
- Model (user): tagging becomes an encoding concern for execution and runtime representation. The custom-tagging assessment remains owned by 20260926-091318.

## Triage

- Tentative syntax (user): `?(term-to-inspect, lhs-binder, rhs-binder) do #|leaf clause|# end do #|stem clause|# end do #|fork clause|# end`. Syntax may change.
- Contract (user): inspect the subject without evaluating it. Resolve identifiers when bound; inspect unbound identifiers as written. Do not force applications or recursively evaluate children.
- Contract (user): in the leaf clause, neither triage binder receives a binding. In the stem clause, only `lhs-binder` receives a binding. In the fork clause, both receive bindings for their respective children.
- Contract (user): executing an unbound identifier is a general language error. Triage of an unbound identifier is permitted. The language still needs an errors section.
