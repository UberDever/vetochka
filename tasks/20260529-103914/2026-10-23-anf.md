## Forcing and extension assessment

- Correction (user, 2026-10-23): `f(g(x))` does not implicitly evaluate its operand in the proposed v0 model. Introducing an automatic binding for `g(x)` would change its semantics. Do not import the paper's call-by-value operand evaluation into Vetochka.
- Model (user): bindings force computations; closures capture their environment. These mechanisms provide the required control over evaluation order.
- Requirement (user): lambdas and bindings belong in the executable core. Assess the other forms in the supplied ANF grammar individually; their presence in an ANF variant does not establish a need for them in v0.
- Proposal (agent, unapproved): include identifiers, literal/tree values, closure application, construction, and structural inspection. Assess whether structural inspection can supply branching without a separate `if`. Keep `call/cc`, `set!`, and primitive `letrec` out of the initial core unless a concrete requirement establishes their need. Native arithmetic and comparison require their own operation contracts, not admission merely because the sample grammar lists them.
- Open (agent): define atomic forms in terms of obtaining values without executing represented code. A represented application can itself be a tree value. The sample grammar's distinction between atomic and compound expressions must be adapted to this representation.
