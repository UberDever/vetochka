## Nyad rename (decision, from ChatGPT dialogue "Naming a node nyad", exported to dialogue/)
- Deltas renamed to nyads; sigil `^` -> `~`; `~` removed from prefix operators.
- Form: `~[]`, `~[x]`, `~[x, y]`; `~[x, y, z]` is a syntax error. Arity structural, no numbers.
- `~` and `$` are analogous sigil heads: entry points into restricted syntactic families. Whitespace after `~` immaterial.
- Applied to `docs/new_spec/02_syntax.md` and `03_v0.md`; old `docs/spec/`, dialogue, memory left in caret notation as history.
